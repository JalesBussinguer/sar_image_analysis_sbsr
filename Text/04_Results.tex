\section{Results and Discussion}

This section presents the results following the same sequence of the experimental design, since the adopted methodology is based in sequential steps.
The results for both NISAR and Sentinel-1 are presented together.

\subsection{Goodness-of-fit tests}

We tested the goodness-of-fit of all 5 samples of each class for the $\Gamma_{SAR}$ and the $\mathcal G^0_I$ models.
Table \ref{tab:gof-rejection} presents the number of samples that rejects the fit on the tested models, according to its mission, class and band.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{ccccc}
\hline
       &       &      & $\Gamma_{SAR}$ & $\mathcal G^0_I$ \\
\cline{4-5}
Mission & Class & Band &  H$_0$ rejections &  H$_0$ rejections \\
\hline
\multirow[c]{6}{*}{NISAR}
  & \multirow[c]{2}{*}{Fo}    & HH & 0 & 0 \\
  &                               & HV & 5 & 0 \\
  & \multirow[c]{2}{*}{Sv}   & HH & 5 & 1 \\
  &                               & HV & 5 & 0 \\
  & \multirow[c]{2}{*}{Gl} & HH & 5 & 1 \\
  &                               & HV & 5 & 0 \\
\hline
\multirow[c]{6}{*}{Sentinel-1}
  & \multirow[c]{2}{*}{Fo}    & VV & 1 & 0 \\
  &                               & VH & 0 & 1 \\
  & \multirow[c]{2}{*}{Sv}   & VV & 2 & 2 \\
  &                               & VH & 0 & 0 \\
  & \multirow[c]{2}{*}{Gl} & VV & 1 & 1 \\
  &                               & VH & 1 & 1 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}%
}
\caption{Fit rejections under $\Gamma_{SAR}$ and $\mathcal G^0_I$ models.}
\label{tab:gof-rejection}
\end{table}

\vspace{0.3cm}

For NISAR, the majority of the samples show a clear fit to the $\mathcal G^0_I$ distribution, since the rejection of fit is restricted to two samples in 30 tests in total, both on HH polarization.
On the other hand, almost all classes rejected the fit for $\Gamma_{SAR}$, except the Forest Class in HH polarization.
This result indicates that these samples are likely to present a full developed speckle.
In contrast, the results for Sentinel-1 do not show a clear fit, since both models presented almost the same number of fit rejection.
However, since $\mathcal G^0_I$ presented less rejections in total, and has tractable and interpretable parameters, we adopted the distribution as the model that better characterizes the samples distributions.
